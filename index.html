    Calcs use each side's listed sets (opponents' tournament sets or their most common set), plain field plus each side's own weather/terrain. Only one Mega can evolve per battle, so bringing two Megas is scored slightly lower.

    Gauntlet

    Plays your team against every tournament and community team in the app ( teams). In each match both sides pick their best 4 with the damage calc, and the score gap becomes a win chance. It's a model, not a battle sim, so use it to compare versions of your team. Evolve keeps swapping unlocked members and sets, and keeps a change only if it beats the field more often. Lock the Pokémon you won't give up.

    Damage calc

    Standard Gen 9 damage formula at Lv 50 with Champions SP (1 SP = +1 stat, checked against in-game stats), 16 rolls, 0.75× spread. Each threat uses its most common set and its own weather/terrain; your Intimidate is applied to physical hits. Crits are off unless the Crits box is ticked (then every hit both ways crits). Not modelled: abilities unique to Champions, weight-based moves (Low Kick/Grass Knot use 80 BP), Foul Play vs invested Atk.
